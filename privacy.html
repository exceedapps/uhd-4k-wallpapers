Privacy Policy

MONARQ Wallpapers

Last updated: 17 September 2026

MONARQ Wallpapers ("the App") is a wallpaper gallery. This policy explains what data is handled when you use the App. We keep it minimal.

Information we do NOT collect

We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or location for our own use. Your favourites are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The App shows an optional rewarded video ad only when you choose to unlock a 4K download. There are no banner ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may process device identifiers and app-interaction data. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Tracking & your choices

On first launch the App asks for App Tracking Transparency permission and, in the EEA/UK, presents a consent form (Google UMP). You can decline. You can change your ad-consent choice any time from Settings › Privacy & Ad Choices in the App, and your tracking choice from the iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Wallpaper content

Wallpapers are delivered from our content server (jsDelivr / GitHub). Loading an image makes a standard web request; no personal data is attached.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
